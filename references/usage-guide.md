# Usage guide: document-expiry-watcher

Ask your NAS what is about to expire.

## Who it is for
Anyone who keeps warranties, insurance policies, permits, licences or contracts on a UGREEN AI NAS and wants to stop missing renewal dates, such as a small business owner or a household.

## How to use it
Say something like: "Check my Insurance folder and tell me what expires in the next 60 days."

You get a report with five groups: Expired, Expiring soon, Later, Date unclear, and No readable date. Each row shows the document, the date as written, and where the file lives.

## What it will and will not do
- It only reads your documents. It never moves, renames, tags or deletes anything.
- It saves the report to your NAS only if you say yes, and the NAS asks for approval first.
- It checks when you ask. It does not send reminders on its own.
- If a date is unclear or a file cannot be read, it tells you instead of guessing.
